We know how it feels.

Waking up every day with pressure in your chest, seeing friends getting placed while you keep refreshing job portals, family questions that hurt more than they intend to, and that constant voice in your head saying "I'm not enough".

You are not lazy. You are exhausted from fighting a system that doesn't see your worth.
Your struggle is real. Your sleepless nights, your silent tears, your hidden anxiety — we see you.

BCP is not just a party. It's a warm corner where you can breathe. Here, we don't judge you for taking time to heal. We celebrate your small wins — whether it's applying to 5 jobs today or just getting out of bed.

Together, we will make this country understand that rest is also productivity, and every unemployed youth is not a burden but a powerhouse waiting for the right opportunity.
