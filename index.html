Manifesto (हमारी मुख्य माँगें)

  1. Judicial Reform Kisi bhi Chief Justice ko retirement ke baad Rajya Sabha ki seat ya koi bhi government position/reward nahi diya jayega.
  2. Electoral Accountability Agar kisi valid citizen ka naam voter list se bina wajah kaata jata hai, toh Chief Election Commissioner (CEC) par UAPA (Terrorism Law) ke tehat case darj hona chahiye.
  3. Women Empowerment Parliament aur Cabinet ki saari seats par mahilaon ko poora 50% arakshan (reservation) milna chahiye.
  4. Media Crackdown Jo bade media houses bade corporates ke hain, unke licenses ko radd kiya jaye aur mainstream news anchors ke bank accounts ki jaanch ho.
  5. Anti-Defection Rule Jo bhi MLA ya MP chunav jeetne ke baad apni party badalta hai, us par 20 saal tak koi bhi chunav ladne ya sarkari pad lene par poora ban ho.
  6. Education System NEET exam me chal rahi dhandhli ke khilaf students ko insaf mile aur CBSE paper re-checking fees turant khatam ho.
